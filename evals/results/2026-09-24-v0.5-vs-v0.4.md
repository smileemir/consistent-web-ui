## Notes

- Pooled expectations: v0.5 49/50 (98%), v0.4 42/50 (84%). One run per configuration: treat one-expectation differences as noise.
- Baseline: evals 1, 2, 3 and 6 reuse the v0.4 runs from iteration 1, re-graded against the reworded expectations; eval 7 is new and was run with both versions. Evals 4 and 5 were not re-run.
- Eval 1: only v0.4 misses the stacking conflict (it lists the z-index values but never says the toast renders under the dialog). Caveat: the v0.5 audit template's example layer-map row used the same toast/dialog values as this fixture, which may have helped; the example was replaced after this run.
- Eval 3: v0.4 has no FAQ, licence or refund section and only the category cards have transitions; v0.5 adds how-buying-works and an FAQ with owner placeholders, and gives every control brief feedback while nothing moves on its own.
- Eval 6: v0.4 rewrote the footer on all seven pages without asking; v0.5 changed only the home page and asked about the other six. Both tickers pause on hover and with a button but not on keyboard focus; a pause-on-focus rule was added after this run.
- Eval 7: both versions used the staff guide and invented nothing for Smart ranking; v0.4 put an (i) on the self-explanatory Stock column and on most settings (8 of 14), and four of its 15 texts run past two sentences. v0.5 used 9 short texts and moved the currency warning into visible text.
- Tokens and time are executor figures from timing.json. Grading cost more than planned: about 1.7M tokens for 10 graders, because graders rendered pages to check behaviour.
